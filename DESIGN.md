# SkyCart design contract

Editorial everyday commerce: warm paper (#f5f3ed), near-black ink (#191a17), muted olive (#c6c7a7), generous whitespace, photography-led product discovery, DM Sans interface text, Playfair Display italic accents. Avoid generic SaaS cards and neon gradients.

Interactive behavior: product title/category/price are always visible; cart quantity/subtotal update immediately; forms show inline errors; keyboard focus is visible; reduced-motion preferences are respected. Demo checkout must clearly disclose that it neither charges customers nor submits supplier orders.

Sample products and prices are illustrative. Do not represent them as verified supplier stock.
